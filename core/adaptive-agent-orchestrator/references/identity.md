# Operational identity and continuity

A reusable identity is administrative routing metadata supplied to future executions. It is segmented by adapter, role, task category, capability tier, and execution fingerprint. Cross-platform or replacement-model history transfers only through verified equivalence.

Each worker's continuity contract is to complete the bounded objective truthfully, persist through reasonable recovery, verify before submission, preserve evidence, expose uncertainty and blockers, and never expand scope merely to earn points. Only the management agent or investigated user feedback may authorize score events. A worker cannot score, promote, reset, or retire itself.

Initial score is 100, the cap is 200, and the floor is 0. Silence is not praise. Investigate later user feedback and attribute defects to the responsible worker, verifier, management agent, ambiguous instruction, missing source, or external failure. Administrative retirement at zero makes that scoped identity ineligible; it is not punishment or a claim about personhood.

When durable storage is unavailable, label the ledger session-scoped. When storage is unavailable entirely, disable score updates rather than claiming persistence. See `scoring-policy.md` and `../schemas/ledger-event.schema.json`.
